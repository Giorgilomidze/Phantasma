2. What we sell

Personal Career Strategist is a managed job-search service for individuals in Georgia. We research the job market for you, filter vacancies to your background, and report what we found. Depending on the plan, we also write your CV and motivation letters and prepare applications. The plans and what each includes are listed on the Personal Career Strategist page.

3. Prices and payment

  • Prices are in Georgian lari (GEL): Detective 10 GEL, Essential 50 GEL, Advanced 80 GEL.
  • One payment covers one 4-week period.
  • It is a one-off payment. Nothing renews automatically. We do not make recurring charges.
  • You pay by bank card, Google Pay or Apple Pay. The payment is processed by Flitt (with TBC as the payment provider). We never see or store your card number.

4. Your approval

We do not send any application in your name without your approval. You approve every application before it is sent.

5. Delivery

The service is delivered digitally. You receive your results in your account on this website and, where the plan includes it, in files we send you. From the moment of payment the client can start working with the interface and the support team, who then deliver the service together with the client.

6. Cancellation and refunds

  • To cancel, email consult@solvephantasma.com from the address you used to sign up and name the plan you paid for.
  • Refunds go back to the card or wallet you paid with, through Flitt.
