HR Assistant

A managed job-search service — we do the searching, writing and applying, you show up to the interviews.

Overview

Looking for a job is a full-time job. The listings are spread across a dozen sites, half of them are already filled by the time you find them, and every serious application wants a CV rewritten for that specific role. Most people simply run out of time and start firing off the same generic CV everywhere.

HR Assistant takes that work off your hands. Every month we search the job boards on your behalf, filter out everything that doesn't match your level and background, rewrite your CV so it actually reads like a fit, write the motivation letters that are needed, and send the applications. You get a running report of every vacancy we found, every application we sent, and where each one stands.

No application is ever sent without your explicit go-ahead. You stay in control of where your name goes — you just don't have to do the legwork.
