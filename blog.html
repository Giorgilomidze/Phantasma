Marketing · AI

AI Advertising Platform

From a shop's stock file to on-brand ad pictures with exact Georgian text, and campaigns that never go live without approval.

Built
Oct – Nov 2026
Status
Live, invite-only

Executive Summary

A web platform, built in-house, that runs Facebook and Instagram advertising for product brands from one place: the product catalogue, a picture library, AI-generated ad pictures in the brand's own styles, a post composer, and campaign set-up with spending limits and approvals. The interface works in English and Georgian.

The Challenge

Small marketing teams spend most of their week on repetitive production: product photos, price banners, the same post in several formats, and a manual check before anything is paid for. General AI image tools help little in Georgia: they do not render Georgian script reliably and they ignore a brand's visual rules.

Technical Solution

Products are imported from the shop's own Excel or CSV stock file (name, quantity, price in GEL); every row is checked and skipped rows are listed by their row number. Each product carries photos and a short note for the AI. Pictures live in a library with folders (uploads, design elements such as price tags and frames, AI-generated, products, archive), tags and a tag filter; archived pictures are removed automatically after 30 days. The brand's picture styles are kept as rules and options that the team edits in the browser, imported from its existing prompt library in Excel. The composer assembles the prompt from those rules, sends the product photo and the chosen style pictures to Google's Gemini image models, and then draws the Georgian product name onto the picture itself, picking a light or dark colour from the banner's brightness, so the text is always exact. Campaigns are set up in a five-step wizard with daily and total budget limits, a typed confirmation before anything goes live and a log of every change; they are validated against Meta's Marketing API in dry-run mode before any money is spent. Access is members-only and enforced in the database (row-level security) and in the server functions, not just in the browser.

Business Impact

Products, pictures and campaigns sit in one searchable place instead of folders, chats and spreadsheets. Georgian text on ad pictures is always correct. Nothing is published or spent without an explicit approval, and every change leaves a record. Built-in limits (30 AI pictures a day, 50 MB of library storage per person) keep running costs predictable.

Technology Stack

  • Google Gemini image models
  • Meta Marketing API
  • Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage)
  • Row-level security
  • Edge Functions (TypeScript)
  • SheetJS
  • Vanilla JavaScript

Tender Analytics Platform

900+ public tenders and 3,800+ documents turned into requirements, deadlines and prices a bidder can act on.

28,642 requirement items extracted
Built
Nov 2026
Access
Paid service, sign-in only

Executive Summary

A web platform for companies that bid in Georgian public procurement. It collects every tender in four categories (furniture, food, hygiene, construction materials) from the state procurement system, reads all of its documents, and turns them into requirements, deadlines, typical prices and competitor histories. It is built as a paid service: data is shown only after sign-in and is kept in private storage.

The Challenge

To find the few tenders worth bidding on, and the price that wins, a bidder has to read each tender's technical specification, price tables and contract draft, often several PDFs and spreadsheets per tender. Past winning prices are scattered across thousands of files. Doing this by hand for hundreds of tenders a month is not possible.

Technical Solution

A resumable collector downloads tenders, bids, contracts and documents in small, polite overnight batches. A document reader (PDF, Word, Excel) extracts each requirement, tags it (for example standards, certificates, laboratory tests), rates the tender's complexity and finds similar past tenders. Bidders' own price tables are read line by line and checked against the bid total. The site offers a dashboard, a tender list with filters, a page per tender (requirements, deadlines, similar tenders, typical prices, market comparison), company and buyer pages with contract timelines, and a Products page with every bidder's line prices. Sign-in with Google; the data sits in private cloud storage that only signed-in users can read.

Business Impact

The full six-month window was collected, plus older tenders: 900+ tenders, with the documents of 920 of them read (3,827 files, 28,642 requirement items, no failures), and 442 companies and 517 buyers profiled. Checked against an expert's own labelling, the reader found 77 of 79 requirements in full and the other 2 in part. Product prices: 855 bidders' files from the last three months, 408 price tables read and 4,084 offer lines; 305 of the tables (75%) add up to the bid total.

Technology Stack

  • Python
  • PDF / Word / Excel parsing
  • Supabase (Auth, Storage)
  • Google sign-in
  • Static site generation

AI CV Reading & Job Matching

A job seeker uploads a CV once; the platform reads it, scores every vacancy against it and applies only where the client agrees.

Built
Sep – Nov 2026
Status
Live

Executive Summary

Personal Career Strategist is an online career service. The client signs in with Google, LinkedIn or email, uploads a CV and answers a preferences questionnaire. The platform reads the CV with AI, finds and scores vacancies, and prepares applications that are sent only after the client ticks them. A client dashboard and an admin panel with live chat run the service day to day.

The Challenge

Job seekers lose hours every week scanning job boards and rewriting the same CV, and many applications go to roles that never fitted. A service that does this for them has to be quick, show why a vacancy fits, and handle personal data with care.

Technical Solution

CV reading: the text is taken out of the PDF in the browser and the name and contact details are removed first; only then does Claude (Anthropic) sort the rest into experience, education, skills and languages. The client sees the result and can turn it into a one-page CV (PDF or JPG). Matching: every vacancy gets a 0–100 score from six weighted factors (job title 45, skills 20, seniority 15, location 10, salary 5, language 5); an unknown salary or language counts half. The dashboard lists companies first, each with its matched vacancies, deadlines and status (chosen, sent, deadline passed, employer replied), plus a 23-question preferences form and downloadable vacancy lists. Applications go out only for vacancies the client approved, and sent ones are locked. The admin panel shows every client's plan, searches and profile completeness, and offers live chat. Plans are paid through a hosted checkout.

Business Impact

The service page shows the live funnel from vacancies found to applications sent. Personal data never reaches the AI model with the client's name or contacts attached, and no application goes out without the client's own approval.

Technology Stack

  • Claude API (Anthropic)
  • Supabase (Auth, PostgreSQL, Storage, Edge Functions)
  • Google / LinkedIn sign-in
  • Python pipeline
  • Flitt payments
  • Vanilla JavaScript

Amazon Listing Monitor

Every product a seller has on amazon.de checked daily, with each change to titles, images and A+ content logged and reported.

935 products checked every day
In production
Since Sep 2026
Run time
~40 min per day

Executive Summary

A read-only monitoring pipeline for a German publisher of gifts and stationery that sells on amazon.de. Every day it pulls what Amazon currently shows for each product, stores a snapshot, logs every change, and writes the result into the team's Google Sheet, with an email summary or alert. It never changes anything on Amazon.

The Challenge

Listing content on Amazon can change without notice: titles, bullet points, images, A+ content and variations are altered by catalogue updates. The seller's team had no way to notice, and checking by hand in Seller Central was not repeatable for about 1,000 products.

Technical Solution

Python on an existing Azure server, with a SQLite database that keeps daily snapshots and a permanent change log. Amazon's Selling Partner API supplies catalogue data, A+ content, seller reports and image dates; requests go in small batches to stay within Amazon's limits and resume automatically after a failure. A self-hosted n8n workflow runs the check every day at 18:00 Berlin time, writes a per-product tab and a per-SKU tab into the Google Sheet with the changes since the last run marked, and sends the email summary or alert. Three handover documents cover the business logic, the technical set-up and day-to-day operation.

Business Impact

935 products (1,873 SKUs) are checked in about 40 minutes per run, with all 935 completed and no failures in the last five recorded runs. A+ content is tracked on 780 of the 935 products. The team sees exactly what changed and when, at no running cost beyond the existing Azure server and Google Workspace.

Technology Stack

  • Python
  • Amazon Selling Partner API
  • SQLite
  • n8n (self-hosted)
  • Google Sheets
  • Azure

Executive Summary

MCG Market (mclub.ge) is a Georgian online marketplace for motorcycles, motorcycle parts and riding gear. It grew out of a Facebook community of about 70,000 members. Our team designed and built the platform.

The Challenge

Buying and selling motorcycles in Georgia happened in a large Facebook group: posts sank in the feed, nothing could be filtered by brand, year or engine size, and parts and gear were mixed in with bikes. The community needed one place that is easy to search and easy to post in.

Technical Solution

A marketplace in three categories (motorcycles, parts, gear) with search and filters by make, model, location, condition, year, engine size and price, and a brand filter for 10 makes. Sellers sign in and post free listings with photos and details in five steps; buyers message sellers through the site. Built with Next.js on Vercel, with images in cloud storage. The platform takes no payments: buyers and sellers deal directly.

Business Impact

The community's trade moved from a social feed to a structured catalogue: every listing carries details the filters can search, and buyers reach sellers without leaving the platform. The customs calculator answers the import-duty question before a purchase.

E-commerce platform

Monolith → Microservices Recovery

PHP monolith decommissioned. Decoupled Azure microservices in its place.

CI/CD governed releases — from debugging marathons lasting weeks
Duration
~6 months
Outcome
Standard Azure-native run rates

Executive Summary

Engaged to perform a forensic audit of a failing e-commerce ecosystem operating across Amazon and Shopify. The client had previously attempted to centralize operations into a single PHP-based monolith. We performed a complete architectural overhaul, replacing the monolith with a modern decoupled microservices cloud architecture.

The Challenge

Every function — ETL processes, API authentications, database procedures, and front-end reporting — existed within one massive codebase. A minor change in one module caused unforeseen regressions elsewhere, leading to debugging marathons lasting weeks. The system was so complex only the original author could navigate it. Reports were hard-coded in Python, requiring senior developers just to change a simple KPI. Single-threaded ETL meant data synchronization lagged as sales volume grew.

Technical Solution

Azure Data Factory replaced hard-coded PHP scripts with visual monitoring of data pipelines, automated retries, and scheduled daily ingestion from Amazon and Shopify APIs. Azure Databricks enabled Spark for massive data processing. Azure SQL centralized all fragmented data into a Single Source of Truth. Power BI empowered non-technical stakeholders to build their own dashboards. Azure DevOps implemented CI/CD pipelines separating Development, Staging, and Production. Data Lake Storage ADLS Gen2 created a Bronze layer storing raw JSON for historical reprocessing. Terraform and Bicep ensured Infrastructure as Code. Azure AD with RBAC secured department-level data access.

Business Impact

Replaced a fragile monolith with a resilient microservices architecture. Operating cost dropped to standard Azure-native run rates. Non-technical stakeholders gained self-service BI. Zero single-point-of-failure in the new architecture. Full CI/CD lifecycle with separated environments.

  • Azure Databricks
  • MSSQL
  • Azure SQL
  • Power BI
  • Azure DevOps
  • Terraform / Bicep
  • Azure AD / Entra ID

Agriculture

Precision Viticulture IoT

Closed-loop irrigation across 12 hectares — river to summit.

12 ha autonomous, weather-aware irrigation
Duration
~9 months incl. agronomic research
Scope
Hardware + cloud + edge support

Executive Summary

Designed and deployed a complete precision agriculture system for a 12-hectare vineyard, combining IoT sensor infrastructure, edge computing, cloud data pipelines, and predictive automation to create a fully autonomous, weather-aware irrigation system.

Research & Agronomic Strategy

Conducted extensive research to define the Optimal Growth Window, establishing specific thresholds for nutrient and soil chemistry including ideal NPK fertilizer ratios, environmental variables covering optimal sunlight exposure and air humidity, the precise moisture level between drought stress and over-saturation to maximize sugar content, and soil pH balance assessments.

Infrastructure & IoT Deployment

Designed hydraulic planning for a system pumping water from a low-altitude river up a mountain slope into storage reservoirs. Specified high-pressure pumps, NPK mixers, water storage tanks, and kilometers of hose droppers for gravity-fed irrigation. Deployed IoT devices in calculated locations to monitor soil NPK, moisture, and temperature in real-time. Built a low-power always-on physical server on-site as a data retrieval center ensuring connectivity in a remote area.

Cloud Architecture & Automation

Built scalable pipelines using Azure and Databricks to sync on-site sensor data with external Weather Forecast APIs. Developed a decision-support model forecasting water needs based on upcoming weather patterns through evapotranspiration versus predicted rainfall. The system automatically controlled the pumping station, keeping the vineyard at optimal moisture while minimizing energy consumption. Result: 12 hectares of autonomous, weather-aware irrigation requiring no manual intervention.

Technology Stack

  • IoT (custom sensors)
  • On-prem edge server
  • Azure Data Factory
  • Azure Databricks
  • Weather APIs
  • Predictive ML

Developed an end-to-end data pipeline and machine learning solution for a large-scale e-commerce distributor managing 12,000 SKUs across 200 brands. Water & Utilities

Predictive Infrastructure for Urban Water Utility

From reactive break-fix to data-driven, predictive operations for a city of 3M+ residents.

+100% daily incident throughput — 40–55 → 80–140 per day
Duration
~6 months
Scope
Discovery, design, build, 6-month support

Executive Summary

A century-old water utility serving a city of more than three million people was running its operations reactively. The SAP ERP incident management module was used as a basic task list, field teams worked without shared performance signals, and IoT data already collected was sitting unused. We redesigned the operational process end-to-end, built a unified data warehouse, deployed a machine-learning forecasting layer, and integrated predictive alerts back into SAP. Daily incident throughput more than doubled, average fix time dropped by 33%, and high-severity pipe bursts fell by 12% year-over-year.

Root Causes Identified

A meaningful portion of the underground network was undocumented or wrongly mapped in SAP, causing field teams to discover wrong pipe specs on-site requiring second trips. Field crews, warehouse staff, and fleet maintenance operated in silos with no shared KPIs — finishing a job quickly was effectively punished. Without forecasting, every road closure request to City Hall arrived as a last-minute emergency. A significant share of pipe failures traced to over-pressure conditions and water hammer effects on sloped heritage infrastructure sections.

Technical Solution

Built a centralized PostgreSQL data warehouse consolidating SAP incident records, live IoT telemetry from pressure gauges and reservoir levels, the asset inventory, and billing-derived consumption map. Surfaced correlations between incidents and air temperature, sediment quantity, geographic zone and elevation, pipe age and material cohort, and in-pipe pressure profiles. Built a machine learning forecasting service achieving 80% daily, 90% weekly, and 93% monthly confidence. Connected forecast-driven alerts back into SAP via custom REST API, generating work orders inside the system operators already used. Power BI dashboards covered incident volume against forecasts, fix-time distribution, team utilization, and warehouse readiness.

Business Impact

Daily incidents resolved increased from 40–55 to 80–140 — a 100–150% improvement. Average fix time reduced from 4.5 hours to 3.0 hours — 33% faster. High-severity pipe bursts fell 12% year-over-year in a controlled comparable period. Activated operational teams increased from approximately 25 to 30 out of 82 total teams. The utility moved from being treated as a problematic neighbor to a data-driven partner in urban management, with improved permit speed and political capital with City Hall.

Technology Stack

  • SAP ERP
  • PostgreSQL
  • Python ML
  • Power BI
  • IoT (pressure & level sensors)
  • Custom REST APIs
